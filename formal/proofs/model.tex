\section*{PABLA-v2 formal model (unreviewed draft)}

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a collector with period indices $p\in\mathbb{N}$,
append-only externally committed checkpoints, and a forward-ratcheted
current state $s_p$. $\mathcal{A}$ is an adaptive PPT attacker that at time
$t^*$ learns the entire current collector state and archive. It may rewrite
or suppress unexternally committed output, but cannot alter final ledger
receipts or an honest witness's durable checkpoints. ``Accept'' means the
actual full verifier has checked a trusted policy including a bounded
heartbeat interval and wall-clock freshness; without that condition the
suffix-truncation counterexample is immediate.

For each event $e$, let $a_e,\sigma_e,\omega_e,\kappa_e$ denote its
arrival, seal, honest witness, and finalized ledger confirmation times;
$\xi_e=\min(\omega_e,\kappa_e)$. Window assertions are predicates over
accepted challenge histories, not mere arithmetic equalities.

Assumptions: collision resistance of the binding tree; forward secrecy of
separately domain-separated PRG evolution and period-secret extraction;
EUF-CMA authentication of period keys; irrevocable key erasure; an honest
witness if witnesses are used; ledger immutability and stated liveness;
serializable WAL commits and crash-idempotent signing.
