\section*{T1 -- External prefix binding (conditional sketch)}
Suppose an adversary supplies two different event prefixes of the same
sequence length that both verify against an identical externally committed
RFC-6962-style root. Distinguish the earliest differing leaf or internal node
in the induced trees: equal parent hashes with distinct children give a
collision in the tree's domain-separated hash, unless the two input encodings
collide. This is a standard Merkle-binding reduction. Authenticated root
origin is an independent assumption: a witness signature or finalized ledger
receipt must be verified before invoking the reduction.

\section*{T2 -- Previous-period forward integrity (conditional sketch)}
At time $t^*$ the adversary receives current state $s_p$. All earlier
states $s_i$ for $i<p$ are assumed erased. Replace the evolution/derivation
PRG outputs up to the challenged prior period by independent random keys
using a hybrid sequence. An accepted new prior-period checkpoint whose
period public key was registered implies an EUF-CMA period signature forgery,
unless a schedule proof or event commitment is broken. A reduction guessing
one of $N$ periods can lose a factor of $N$, plus the sum of PRG distinguisher
advantages.
